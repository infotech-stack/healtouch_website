Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your personal information

Information We Collect

At healtouch Physio & Rehab Centre, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you book an appointment, contact us, or use our services. This may include your name, phone number, email address, medical history, and any other information you choose to share.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our physiotherapy and rehabilitation services, process appointments, communicate with you about your care, send appointment reminders, and respond to your inquiries. We may also use your information for internal record keeping and to improve our services.

Medical Information Confidentiality

As a healthcare provider, we are committed to protecting your medical information in accordance with applicable laws. Your health information is kept strictly confidential and is only shared with your treating physiotherapists and staff directly involved in your care. We do not share your medical information with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

WhatsApp Communication

When you choose to communicate with us via WhatsApp, please note that WhatsApp is a third-party platform with its own privacy policies. We use WhatsApp solely for appointment scheduling and communication. We recommend not sharing sensitive medical information via WhatsApp.

Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and improve our services. You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent for certain data processing activities. To exercise these rights, please contact us at care@healtouch.in.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
